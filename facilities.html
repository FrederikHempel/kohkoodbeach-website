Facilities & Service

Everything you need

Complimentary tea and coffee from 07:30 to 22:00 in the restaurant, free wi-fi throughout, and fins for snorkelling you can borrow at reception against a deposit. Kayaking and volleyball are on the beach whenever you want them.

Dining

Restaurant

Room for 60 guests, free wi-fi, and chefs who cook local dishes from produce picked out at the market each morning. Eat with the sea breeze coming through the open-air room and a panoramic view in front of you.

Opening hours
07:30 – 21:00
Relax

Swimming Pool

Surrounded by the nature you only get on Koh Kood — the beach front on one side, coconut trees on the other. A kids' pool at 0.5 m depth sits alongside the adult pool, so swimming works for the whole family.

Opening hours
08:00 – 18:00
Kids' pool depth
0.5 m
