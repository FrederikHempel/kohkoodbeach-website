Questions, answered

The simplest route is the Boonsiri combined ticket: a VIP bus from Bangkok to Laem Sok pier (about 5–6 hours), then a high-speed catamaran to Ao Salad pier on Koh Kood (about 1 hour). We collect you at Ao Salad pier and drive you to the resort. You can also fly from Bangkok to Trat and continue to the pier by minivan, though the connection is not always same-day and costs a good deal more. See our Getting Here page for the full timetable and fares.

Yes. Our Thai Twin House and Bali House bungalows are designed to fit families, we have a shallow kids' pool (0.5 m depth), and extra beds are available in every room style. See our Family Stays page for details and child pricing.

November to April is high season — the driest, sunniest weather, around 30–35°C. Green season (roughly June to September) is quieter with lower rates, but ferry services run less frequently, so we recommend contacting us to confirm the current timetable before booking flights.

There's no online payment on this website. Send an enquiry with your dates and party size, and we'll confirm availability and rates by email, then arrange payment directly with you.

All rates are quoted in Thai Baht (THB). We show an approximate EUR/DKK/SEK/GBP conversion on our Accommodation and Getting Here pages as a rough guide — please check a current exchange rate before booking, as rates fluctuate.

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available at the restaurant, right beside your room.

Breakfast is included for the standard occupancy of each bungalow (2 guests for Bali House styles, 4 guests for Bali Deluxe and Thai Twin House). Breakfast for a child aged 2–11 can be added for 200 THB per person. Check each room's amenity list on the Accommodation page for full details.

Booking directly through this website, by phone, or by email means no third-party commission is added to your rate.

Yes, an extra bed for a child or adult is available in every bungalow style for a surcharge of 800 THB per person.

Yes — we can arrange a private minivan or car from Bangkok, Suvarnabhumi, or Trat airport to Laem Sok pier, and help with ferry ticket reservations. Please contact us at least 30 days in advance during high season.

Still have a question? Get in touch — we reply within 24 hours, usually the same day.
