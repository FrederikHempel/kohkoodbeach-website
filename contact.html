Privacy and cookies

Who we are. Koh Kood Beach Resort, 121 Moo 1, Koh Kood District, Trat 23000, Thailand. For any privacy question, write to reservation@kohkoodbeachresorts.com.

What we store, and why

Strictly necessary. One item, saved in your browser only: your cookie choice itself, so we don't ask again on every page. This is saved regardless of what you choose, because remembering the choice is what lets us respect it.

Marketing — only with your consent. If you accept, we load the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram). It sets a cookie (_fbp) and may recognise you if you are already logged into Facebook in the same browser, so Meta can tell us which of our Facebook or Instagram campaigns led to an enquiry. That data is shared with Meta Platforms Ireland Limited under Meta's own terms — see Meta's Privacy Policy for how long they keep it and what they do with it.

We do not use any other tracking, analytics or advertising tool on this site.

Your choice

You can accept or decline the marketing cookie at any time — use "Cookie settings" in the footer of any page to change your mind. Declining does not affect your ability to browse the site or send us an enquiry.
