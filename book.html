Getting here from Bangkok

Boonsiri Bus + High Speed Ferry

One combined ticket covering the bus from Bangkok and the ferry across. A VIP air-conditioned bus leaves Boonsiri's Khaosan Road office or Rama 9 for Laem Sok pier (about 5–6 hours), where the catamaran continues to Ao Salad pier (about 1 hour). Book at boonsiriferry.com.

05:00 → 11:45 / 08:00 → 16:00

Private Minivan

A private car or minivan can collect you from any Bangkok hotel and drive straight to Laem Sok pier — about 5 hours (320 km) depending on traffic. Please book at least 30 days ahead.
