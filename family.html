Family Stays

Several of our bungalow styles are built for parents and children to share a room comfortably, and the pool and private beach give younger guests space to play close by.

Where to stay with children

2 adults + up to 3 children

Bali House

A 46 m² cottage with a king bed and a sofa bed for extra guests — suits smaller children sharing with parents.

 4 adults + 2 children (0–7 years)

Thai Twin House

Twin 63 m² bungalows sharing a terrace and jacuzzi — the roomiest option for a family of four or more.

Made for younger guests too

Kids' pool

A separate shallow pool at 0.5 m depth alongside the main pool, open 08:00–18:00.

Private beach

Steps from your bungalow, with kayaking and volleyball for the whole family.

Extra beds

Available in every bungalow style for a child or adult, at a fixed surcharge.

As with any beach holiday, please supervise children in and around the pool and the sea at all times — our staff are happy to point out the calmest spots to swim.
