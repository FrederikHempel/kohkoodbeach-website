Koh Kood Beach Resort

Stay closer to nature

An unspoiled island hideaway in the Koh Chang Marine Park

Koh Kood Beach Resort sits on a quiet stretch of private beach on Thailand's fourth largest island — still unspoiled, still slow, still the kind of place where the loudest thing is the sea.

Bali-style bungalows are set among coconut trees, each with an open-air bathroom and a terrace of its own. Bike to the fishing villages, swim beneath Klong Chao waterfall, or simply stay put with a cold beer and watch the light go.

Read our story

Four bungalow styles, one private beach, and a staff who have looked after guests here for years — set inside a protected marine park, an hour by boat from the mainland.

Highlights & Experiences

All activities
01.

Klong Chao Waterfall

A thirty-minute drive and a swim in the clear basin below the falls. Life jackets and guides provided — 250 THB per person.

 02.

Snorkelling at Koh Rang

Shallow coral gardens and a great diversity of tropical fish in the marine park — speedboat, equipment and lunch included.

 03.

Thai Massage

Genuine Thai herbal massage from therapists trained in traditional technique, right here at the resort. 500–1,000 THB.

 04.

The Open-Air Restaurant

Local dishes from produce picked up at the market each morning, eaten with the sea breeze coming through. Open 7:30–21:00.

 05.

Scuba Diving

Take your Open Water certification in our own pool, then join our partner Paradise Divers out to the wrecks and reef — hard corals, sea whips and the occasional barracuda.

The Journey

Getting here is half of what keeps it quiet

A Boonsiri VIP bus runs from Bangkok down to Laem Sok pier in five to six hours. A high-speed catamaran crosses to Ao Salad in about one. We meet you at the pier — roughly seven hours, door to door.

Full timetable & fares →
  1. 01

    Bus from Bangkok

    A Boonsiri VIP bus runs from Khaosan Road or Rama 9 to Laem Sok pier in about 5–6 hours.

  2. 02

    High-speed ferry

    A catamaran crosses from Laem Sok to Ao Salad pier on Koh Kood in about an hour.

  3. 03

    Pickup to the resort

    We meet you at Ao Salad pier and drive you the last stretch to the beach.
