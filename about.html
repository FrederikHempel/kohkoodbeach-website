About the resort

Still an unspoiled and very quiet spot

Indulge yourself and experience the laid-back, luxurious ambience in the lush surroundings of Koh Kood Beach Resort. The island inspires you to cool down, sit back, and watch the stunning sunsets.

Located on Thailand's fourth largest island inside the Koh Chang Marine Park, the resort invites you to explore the unique nature, the white sandy beaches, and the crystal-clear water. The welcoming staff will make you feel special throughout your stay.

Bike around the island to appreciate the serene nature and the peaceful way of fishing village life at Salad Bay and Big Bay — and don't miss Klong Chao Waterfall.
