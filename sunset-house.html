Sunset House

Currently under renovation

Sunset House cannot be booked at this time. We have not announced a reopening date. Please choose one of our available bungalow styles for your stay.

Explore available rooms

A house with a sea view

Before renovation, Sunset Upper offered two individual bedrooms opening onto a shared terrace, with a shared jacuzzi and views towards the sea. It was designed for families or friends staying together.

The previous layout included a king bed and a double bed, open-air bathrooms, air conditioning, a television, tea and coffee facilities, and a minibar. The description here refers to the house before renovation; the final layout and facilities will be confirmed when it reopens.

Planning a stay in the meantime?

Explore Bali Deluxe for a king bed and two singles, or Thai Twin House for two rooms with a shared terrace and jacuzzi.

For questions about Sunset House, contact our team. We are not accepting Sunset House reservations during renovation.
