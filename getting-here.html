Getting Here

Koh Kood sits close to the Cambodian border inside the Koh Chang Marine Park — quieter and more pristine than any other island in Thailand, largely because getting here takes a little effort. The best months are November to April, when it sits around 30–35°C.

01

Bus from Bangkok

A Boonsiri VIP bus from Khaosan Road or Rama 9 to Laem Sok pier, about 5–6 hours.

02

High-speed ferry

A high-speed catamaran from Laem Sok pier to Ao Salad pier on Koh Kood, about 1 hour.

03

Pickup to the resort

We meet you at Ao Salad pier and drive you the last stretch to the resort.

Ao Salad Pier

Roughly seven hours door to door from Bangkok.

Prices are in Thai Baht (THB). As a rough guide only, 1,000 THB is approximately 26 EUR / 195 DKK / 240 SEK / 24 GBP — exchange rates move, so please check a current converter before booking.
01.

Boonsiri Bus + High Speed Ferry Recommended

One combined ticket covering the bus from Bangkok and the ferry across. A VIP air-conditioned bus leaves Boonsiri's Khaosan Road office or Rama 9 for Laem Sok pier (about 5–6 hours), where the catamaran continues to Ao Salad pier (about 1 hour). Book at boonsiriferry.com.

Total journey is roughly 7–8 hours. A snack box, water and one meal are included. Tell us your arrival time and we will meet you at Ao Salad pier.

02.

Private Minivan

A private car or minivan can collect you from any Bangkok hotel and drive straight to Laem Sok pier — about 5 hours (320 km) depending on traffic. Please book at least 30 days ahead.

03.

Public Bus

The most cost-saving route: a public bus from Ekkamai or the Northern Bus Terminal (Mo Chit) to Trat. Arrive at Trat bus station before 23:00 for a same-day transfer, and reserve the ferry in advance — the ferry company arranges a minivan from platform 25 to Laem Sok, connecting with the 12:30 crossing.

Ekkamai: 0-2391-2504, 0-2391-8097 · Mo Chit: 0-2537-8055, 0-2936-2852-66

04.

By Car

Take the motorway route — Banbung, Klang, Chanthaburi, Trat — roughly 4.5–5 hours.

05.

Flying via Trat

There is a small airport at Trat, served from Bangkok, and the flight itself takes about an hour. It still leaves roughly an hour by road to Laem Sok pier and an hour by boat, and the timings do not always connect on the same day. Most guests find the bus and ferry simpler, and it costs a good deal less. We are glad to arrange the pier transfer whichever way you travel.
