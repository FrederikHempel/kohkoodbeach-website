Activities

Once you have felt it

A few things worth doing while you are here. To book any of them, speak to us directly or call +66 (0) 8 1908 8966.

01.

Thai Massage

Our therapists have completed a Thai Therapeutic Techniques and Thai Herbal Massage course, and know how to pick the right herb for you.

Open
09:00 – 17:00
Rate
500 – 1,000 THB
02.

Klong Chao Waterfall

A minivan runs you both ways for the thirty-minute drive. Stones surround the falls and a big basin of fresh clean water sits below for a swim. Life jackets and staff throughout.

Open
10:00 – 15:00
Rate
250 THB / person (min. 4)
03.

Snorkelling at Koh Rang

An underwater world of shallow coral reefs and a great diversity of tropical fish at the well-known marine park. Book at least 3 days ahead in high season.

Open
10:00 – 14:00
Rate
1,100 THB / person, all in
04.

Scuba Diving

Our partner "Paradise Divers" runs the course and shares their knowledge of the marine life here — hard corals, sea whips, damselfish, larger pelagic fish and barracuda.

Booking
Contact reception
05.

Rent a Motorbike

The roads are paved for a comfortable ride to Klong Chao Waterfall, Klong Chao Beach, Big Bay, a fishing village, and much more.

Open
08:00 – 18:00
Rate
250 THB / day (excl. fuel)
